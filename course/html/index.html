Mini PRAGMA — an interactive course

A hello-world Transformer course, built around Revolut's PRAGMA paper. Every lesson has a runnable script, a line-by-line walkthrough, and an interactive HTML page (this collection). Designed to teach a curious teenager — or curious adult — from zero.

How to use this site. Read each lesson in order. The interactive widgets are the heart of it — slide them around, click the buttons, watch what happens. You learn more from 30 seconds of playing than from 5 minutes of reading.

📖 The lessons

#TitleWhat you'll learn
L1 How a model learns What "training" actually means. Watch a 2-knob model discover y = 2x + 1.
L1b Architecture vs. training The single most important distinction in ML, separated cleanly.
L1c Inside gradient descent What loss.backward() and opt.step() actually do. With a roll-the-ball demo.
L2 Tokens & embeddings How words become number-lists. Drag words around in 2D space.
L3 Attention The big idea of the Transformer. Build an attention matrix word by word.
L3b Why Transformers won Side-by-side animated comparison of RNN (sequential) vs Transformer (parallel).
L4 Training a tiny BERT The fill-in-the-blank game. Mask a word, predict it back. Live demo.
L5 pragma_mini.py walkthrough Reading every line of the toy with full understanding. Plus what real PRAGMA adds.
L5b Streaming churn predictor Same recipe, applied to a realistic problem. Full pre-train → freeze → probe pipeline.
L6 Capstone: mini fraud detector You build the workflow yourself. The project.
