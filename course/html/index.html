Mini PRAGMA — learn how AI really works

🎯 Your mission

Computers can't read. They only do math on numbers. By the end of this course you'll know exactly how a pile of numbers learns to play fill-in-the-blank — the same trick behind ChatGPT — and you'll build your own little AI fraud detective from scratch.

No math degree needed. Just basic Python and curiosity. Every lesson has something to click, drag, or run — you learn more in 30 seconds of playing than 5 minutes of reading.

🧱 The one program you build: mini.py

You start by writing one line — tip = 0.2 × bill — and end by building a real, working language model: a tiny transformer, the same kind of machine as BERT and ChatGPT, just small enough to read every line of.

What it processes: a sentence with one word hidden — dog ___. What it learns: to guess the missing word. To win that fill-in-the-blank game it has to teach itself which words belong together — that dog goes with bark, cat with meow — discovering the structure of its little language from nothing but examples, the same way ChatGPT learns English from the internet. Nobody writes those rules down; the model finds them.

Under the hood it's the whole pipeline — words → embeddings → attention → a prediction head — trained by gradient descent on an autograd engine you build too. Pure Python, no PyTorch. One file, grown one rung per lesson — watch for the indigo 🧱 mini.py boxes.

  • See it run — the trained transformer answering for real — at Lesson 4, Step 9.
  • Build it from scratch and train it live, cell by cell, in the notebook lesson_mini.ipynb.
  • The finished file: course/mini.py — run python3 mini.py and it learns all three rules in ~4 seconds.
