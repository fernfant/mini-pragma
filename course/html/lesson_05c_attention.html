Step 2 — Attention: words mix meaning

Now the words can look at each other. The word doing the predicting builds one blend — a weighted mix of all the words — and you get to set the weights yourself.

overview Whole flow step 1 Encode step 2 Attention step 3 Neurons step 4 Output

For once upon a, the focus word pulls the three words in at 30% / 27% / 43% and mixes them into one new vector — the blend. Below, you set those percentages yourself.

After encoding, the words are just sitting in a row, each minding its own numbers. Attention is where they finally talk. The last word — the one about to predict what comes next, which we'll call the focus word — reaches back to every word (including itself) and pulls in a share of each. The result is a single new vector, the blend. Drag the dials below and watch the blend change in real time.

Predict You control the blend

How Where the weights come from

The model doesn't pick the mix by hand. It scores how relevant each word is to the focus word (by comparing their vectors), then squashes those scores into percentages that add to 100% with softmax — the same squasher you'll meet again at the very end. For "once upon a", the focus word is "a", and the real numbers are:

relevance scores:   once 0.10   upon 0.00   a 0.47
softmax →           once  30%   upon  27%   a 43%

blend = 0.30·[once] + 0.27·[upon] + 0.43·[a]
      = [0.18, 0.42, -0.03, -0.09, -0.05, 0.31]

So the blend leans most on "a" (43%) but still carries a chunk of "once" and "upon". That one vector now stands in for the whole phrase so far — and it's what the next stage actually thinks about.

Play Break it on purpose

Set the dials so the focus word ignores itself and listens only to "once". The blend shifts, and if you follow the dimmed pipeline rightward, the guess can change too. That's the whole point of attention: which words you mix decides what the model thinks comes next. Real Transformers run this same step many times in parallel — each copy, called a head, tracks a different kind of relationship (grammar, meaning, reference) — so a word can follow all of them at once.

✅ You can now say…
  • Attention builds one blend: a weighted mix of every word's vector.
  • The weights come from relevance scores → softmax (they add to 100%).
  • Change the weights and you change what the rest of the network sees — and predicts.
