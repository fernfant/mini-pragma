How these ratings were made

Each concept was cold-read by a Karpathy-style reviewer pretending to be a 13-year-old with zero ML knowledge, reading top-to-bottom for the first time. The score rates the introduction: is there a concrete example before any jargon? Is the word defined before it's used? One idea at a time? A picture for anything spatial? Most of the course rates 4–5; the few 3s flag spots that could be made even gentler.
