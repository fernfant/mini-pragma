Seeing What each stage is doing

Here's the whole pipeline in words — and each stage has its own zoom-in page above if you want to slow down and play with it:

  • Words come in. Three tokens, with the last one ringed — that's the focus word doing the predicting.
  • They become numbers. Each word lights up as its embedding: a short column of values, colour showing sign and size.
  • Attention routes meaning. The focus word pulls a weighted mix of every word into one blend. The percentages are a real softmax over the word-to-word scores.
  • Neurons fire. The blend runs through a layer of neurons; ReLU zeroes the ones that don't activate, leaving a sparse pattern — the "thinking".
  • The answer blooms. That pattern becomes scores over candidate next-words; softmax squashes them to add to 100%, and the model commits to the brightest.
Is the guess real? Yes. This tiny network was actually trained (the same predict → measure error → nudge loop from Lesson 1) on these four phrases, so at the default weights each one predicts its true next word — once upon a → time, the sky is → blue. It's a toy with a 5-word vocabulary, not GPT, but the flow and the numbers are honest. Now drag a dial to 100% and watch the blend, the firing, and the guess all shift — that's why attention matters.
✅ You can now say…
  • A forward pass is one left-to-right trip: encode → attention → feed-forward → output.
  • Attention builds a weighted blend of the words; change the weights and you change what the rest of the network sees.
  • The output is a probability distribution over next words — the model picks the highest.
